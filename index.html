How can we help?

AI Companion: Chats & Stories

Contact support

Email predpriyatie143@gmail.com or contact support on Telegram. Include your app version and the Account ID shown under Settings → Account. Never send passwords or full payment details.

Restore purchases

Open Profile → Settings → Restore purchases, or use Restore purchases on a purchase screen. Sign in with the Apple Account used for the purchase. Apple can restore active subscriptions and unfinished purchases; spent consumable Coins or Opals are not restored. Delivered balances and Bond Pass branches are stored in your guest account.

Manage a subscription

Open Apple subscription settings to change or cancel your plan. Deleting the app or its guest account does not cancel Apple billing.

Account and privacy

The app uses a guest account secured by this installation. Keep the installation to retain access to your chats and creations. Before deleting an account, cancel Apple billing if needed and read the final confirmation. Profile → Settings → Account → Delete account permanently removes its data. Details are in our Privacy Policy.

AI and safety

All characters are fictional adults. AI replies may be inaccurate or unexpected. Report and block a character from its profile; published creations require moderation. For an urgent safety or copyright concern, contact the support email above with the character name and details.
