Privacy Policy

In short: we use the information needed to provide AI chats, generated media, purchases, account security, and notifications. We do not sell personal data, show third-party ads, or track you across other companies’ apps and websites.

Who we are

This policy describes how AI Companion: Chats & Stories (the “App”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use the App. Questions may be sent to predpriyatie143@gmail.com.

Information we process

  • Account and security data: a pseudonymous guest account ID, device-attestation identifiers and keys, security tokens, and limited technical request data used to authenticate the installation and prevent abuse.
  • User content: chat messages and prompts, custom-character settings, generated photos and videos, and related generation status.
  • App activity: character progress, likes, reminder state, daily rewards, and coin balance.
  • Purchases: product and App Store transaction identifiers, subscription status, and purchase delivery state. Apple processes payment details; we do not receive your full card or bank details.
  • Notifications: an Apple Push Notification service token if you grant notification permission.

Your profile name, date of birth, interests, preferences, and saved family invitations are stored by our service to provide the features you choose. The App is intended only for adults aged 18 or older.

How we use information

We process this information to operate chats and media generation; save your conversations, characters, progress, and content; deliver coins and subscription access; send notifications you enabled; maintain security; prevent duplicate purchase credit and abuse; troubleshoot service failures; and comply with legal obligations.

Service providers

Information is shared only as needed to provide the service. Before your first AI chat or image request, the app asks for permission to send the content listed below to external AI providers. You can withdraw that permission under Settings → Allow AI data processing:

  • Apple: StoreKit purchases, App Attest, DeviceCheck, and push notifications.
  • OpenRouter and configured text-model providers: chat prompts and recent conversation context used to generate character responses.
  • WaveSpeed and configured media-model providers: prompts and a selected character reference image used to create photos or videos (currently including OpenAI image and Alibaba Wan video models).
  • Our hosting provider: encrypted delivery, security, and hosting of the API, database, and generated media.

These providers process data under their own terms and privacy commitments and may process it in countries outside yours. We do not permit them to use App data for advertising on our behalf.

Retention and account deletion

Account content is retained while your guest account exists. Under Profile → Settings → Account → Delete account permanently, you can erase your profile, conversations, saved memories, characters, invitations, generated media and account credentials regardless of your currency balance. Deletion starts after your final confirmation and cannot be undone. File cleanup is recorded durably and retried if storage is temporarily unavailable. Content you shared with another account may remain in that account’s saved conversations.

For privacy questions or a manual request, contact predpriyatie143@gmail.com and provide your guest account ID. Deletion is also available directly in the app.

Deletion does not cancel an Apple subscription; subscriptions are managed in your Apple Account settings. We may retain a minimal, account-free record of a consumed App Store transaction identifier to prevent the same consumable purchase from being credited again, and information that must be kept for legal, fraud-prevention, or accounting obligations. Operational security logs are retained only as reasonably necessary.

Security

Connections use HTTPS. The App uses Apple App Attest and short-lived credentials to protect guest accounts. No storage or transmission system is completely secure, but we use technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the service.

Your choices and rights

You may disable notifications in iOS Settings, manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account, and request account deletion in the App or through our privacy contact. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to request access, correction, restriction, objection, or deletion. Contact us at the email above to exercise a right or ask a privacy question.

Children

The App is for adults aged 18 or older and is not directed to children. If you believe a minor has provided information, contact us so we can investigate and delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy when the App or its providers change. The effective date above identifies the current version. Material changes will be communicated as required by applicable law.
