Account deletion

AI Companion: Chats & Stories · Updated October 8, 2026

Delete from the app

  1. Open Profile → Settings → Account.
  2. Choose Delete account permanently.
  3. Read the confirmation and confirm deletion.

Deletion removes your profile, conversations, saved memories, custom characters, invitations, generated media, unused currency and account credentials. It cannot be undone. Media cleanup is retried if storage is temporarily unavailable. Content shared with another account may remain in that account’s saved conversations.

Apple subscriptions

Deleting an account does not cancel Apple billing. Manage or cancel your subscription in Apple subscription settings.

Request help

If you cannot open the app, contact predpriyatie143@gmail.com with your guest Account ID if available. Never send passwords, full payment details or private chat content. The app uses an installation-secured guest account; a support request may need ownership verification.

A minimal account-free record of consumed App Store transaction identifiers may remain to prevent duplicate purchase credit, along with records required for legal or fraud-prevention obligations. See the Privacy Policy for retention and data handling.
